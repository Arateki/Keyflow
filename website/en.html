01 — The orb

Tap. Speak. It's written.

  1. 01

    Tap the orb

    It floats over any app — or lives in the keyboard's mic key. It only records when you say so.

  2. 02

    Speak your way

    Pause, resume, cancel or undo with a gesture. The timer runs in the center of the orb.

  3. 03

    The text lands in the field

    Right where your cursor is: a message, an email, a note or a search.

04 — Privacy

What you type stays on your phone.

A keyboard sees everything. That's why KeyFlow is built to keep the minimum and send out only what you ask for.

Read the privacy policy →

  • Your keystrokes never leave the phone

    Typed text goes straight to the app you're using. Nothing is logged or sent.

  • The mic only listens when you speak

    Recording starts and stops when you decide.

  • Truly offline

    With on-device models, transcription works in airplane mode.

  • Your keys, protected

    API keys and logins are encrypted by the Android Keystore. No KeyFlow sign-up.

This page too: no cookies, no analytics, no trackers. Everything is served from here.
